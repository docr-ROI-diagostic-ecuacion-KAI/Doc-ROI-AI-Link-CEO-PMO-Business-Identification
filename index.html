02 · Business Process Definition

Project Management Office (PMO) · Business Identification · SIPOC

One accountable process and five steps. Main SIPOC customer: Reputational Commitment; other contracts go to their designated recipients.

Suppliers

D · Data

Business owner / CEO; existing authorised project records and documented information sources.

Inputs

I · Information

Project, sector, origin, target market, business model, planning unit, product/service identity and an optional initial Lead User hypothesis.

Process

I · Intelligence

Collect one answer at a time, structure declarations, explore a Lead User candidate, check completeness and provenance, and request confirmation.

Outputs

I · Insights

Business Identification Sheet, Lead User Candidate Sheet, validation report and portable, versioned JSON documents.

Customers

P · Personalisation

Main next process: Affairs & Risk · Reputational Commitment (Values + Quality). Other information recipients: Risk; Strategy Research. PMO coordinates delivery status.

Process boundary: Business Identification establishes the business context and may record an initial Lead User hypothesis; it does not approve a selling price, Buyer Persona, financial budget or corporate strategy. Information remains a user declaration until independently verified where required. SIPOC defines accountability; DIIIP describes the transformation of business information.

METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

DIIIP explains how business context becomes executive value

PMO coordinates the business record. Reputational Commitment is the main next SIPOC customer; Risk and Strategy Research receive relevant shared context. Lead User and product hypotheses remain research inputs, not validated results.

D
Data

The business owner, existing project records and documented sources provide initial declarations and supporting evidence.

I
Information

The business idea is organised into project name, sector, origin, target market, business model, planning unit and offer identity.

I
Intelligence

The laboratory checks completeness, field provenance, consistency and human confirmation without inventing missing values.

I
Insights

The Business Identification Sheet, validation status and downloadable business record expose what is known and what still requires confirmation.

P
Personalization Actions

The confirmed business context can be prepared for Reputational Commitment and made available to Risk and Strategy Research. Saving directly to a company database is not yet available in this educational Pill.
